About EstateIQ & Architecture

MLOps Foundation
📊

California Housing Dataset

Derived from the 1990 U.S. Census, containing 20,640 block groups across California. Target variable represents median house value in $100,000 units. Note that in the original 1990 census, values above $500,000 were top-coded.

⚙️

Feature Engineering

Computes domain ratios: bedrooms_per_room (ratio of bedrooms to total rooms), rooms_per_household, and population_per_household. Scaled via Scikit-learn StandardScaler within an isolated pipeline to prevent data leakage.

🔬

Models & MLflow Tracking

Compares HistGradientBoostingRegressor and RandomForestRegressor. Experiments, hyperparameters, artifacts, and test metrics are logged to mlruns/ under the EstateIQ_House_Price_Experiment.

🛡️

Grounded Uncertainty

Instead of heuristic confidence intervals, EstateIQ uses empirical out-of-sample Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) to produce defensible ±1 standard residual error prediction boundaries.
